# Answers for App Store Connect

Every question App Store Connect asks, answered, in the order it asks them. Copy these in.

`docs/APP-STORE.md` is the reasoning. This is the form-filling, extracted so submission day
is transcription rather than decision-making, and so the answers are reviewable in a diff
before they are typed into a web form that keeps no history.

## 2. Age rating questionnaire

Answer **No** to every content question. The two that need thought:

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Medical/Treatment Information | **No** | Cairn is educational self-help. It makes no diagnostic or treatment claim, and the description says so explicitly. Answering Yes here invites 1.4.1 scrutiny for a claim the app does not make. |
| Unrestricted Web Access | **No** | The app opens three URLs (privacy, terms, disclaimer) and they go to Safari via `Linking.openURL`. There is no in-app browser — `__tests__/safety.test.mjs` asserts no WebView exists anywhere in the source. |
| Horror/Fear, Violence, Profanity, Sexual Content, Gambling, Contests, Drugs, Alcohol | **No** | None present. |

**Expected result: 4+.** Note that the age rating is not the same as the audience. This app
is written for adults and older teenagers, and `legal/` treats 16 as the floor; the rating
reflects content, not suitability judgement.

**If the questionnaire produces something higher than 4+,** stop and find out which answer
caused it rather than accepting it — an inflated rating costs reach for nothing.

## 3. App Privacy ("nutrition label")

**At v1, before RevenueCat is wired: `Data Not Collected`.** Every category, no exceptions.

That answer is true and is verifiable from the source: no analytics SDK, no crash reporter,
no attribution SDK, no network call of any kind. It is also one of the most valuable assets
this listing has, and it is worth understanding exactly what will break it.

### What changes the day RevenueCat ships

RevenueCat is a data processor, and its presence must be declared **in the same submission
as the build that contains it** — not afterwards.

| Data type | Linked to identity? | Used for tracking? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase History | No | **No** | App Functionality |
| User ID | No | **No** | App Functionality |

"User ID" is RevenueCat's anonymous `$RCAnonymousID`, which is generated by the SDK and is
not linked to any identity because the app has no accounts to link it to.

**"Used for tracking" must stay No,** which requires that three RevenueCat APIs are never
called: `collectDeviceIdentifiers()`, `enableAdServicesAttributionTokenCollection()`, and
any `setAttributionData`. Calling any of them makes the answer Yes, requires an ATT prompt,
and ends the "no tracker" claim.

### App Tracking Transparency

**Not required, and must not be added.** ATT applies to tracking across apps and websites
owned by other companies. Cairn does none, and an ATT prompt when you are not tracking is a
rejection risk in itself.

## 4. Export compliance

**Uses non-exempt encryption: No.** Already declared in `app.json` as
`ios.config.usesNonExemptEncryption: false`, so App Store Connect will stop asking per build.

The app does encrypt its local storage (XChaCha20-Poly1305, key in the Keychain), but
encryption used solely to protect data on the user's own device falls within the standard
exemption. This is the ordinary answer for an app in this shape.

## 5. Pricing and availability

- **Availability: all territories.** See `docs/LOCALISATION.md` §1.
- **Primary language: English (U.S.).** One locale, worldwide availability — a normal
  combination, and the reasoning is in `docs/LOCALISATION.md` §3.
- **Category: Health & Fitness.** Never Medical, which invites 1.4.1 review and buys nothing.
- **Small Business Program: enrol.** 15% commission instead of 30%, takes minutes, and every
  revenue figure in `docs/GROWTH.md` assumes it.

### In-app purchases

| Reference name | Display name | Type | Price | Trial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `steady_plus_yearly` | Cairn+ Yearly | Auto-renewable subscription | $79.99/year | 1 month free |
| `steady_plus_monthly` | Cairn+ Monthly | Auto-renewable subscription | $12.99/month | 1 month free |
| `steady_plus_onetime` | Cairn+ One-Time | Non-consumable | $149 | — |

Display names are indexed by App Store search, so they are worth getting right, and they are
shown in the purchase sheet, so they have to stay honest.

**Never name the one-off product "Lifetime".** App Review rejects it on the grounds that no
developer can guarantee content for a customer's life. `__tests__/safety.test.mjs` and
`__tests__/store-metadata.test.mjs` both keep the word out of the app and the listing; the
App Store Connect field is the one place no test can reach, so it is on you.

**The trial must be configured as "1 month".** App Store Connect sells introductory offers in
fixed durations only — 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 2 months, 3 months, 6 months,
1 year. `PRICING.trialDays` is 30 to match, and a test asserts it stays inside that set.
